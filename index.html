About

About me

I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing at Amirkabir University of Technology, supervised by Dr. Zahed Rahmati and Prof. Amir Goharshady . I am a member of the ALPACAS research group (Algorithms, Logic, Program Analysis, Cryptocurrencies and Smart Contracts).

My current research interests include parameterized algorithms, SAT/SMT solving, computational geometry, and graph algorithms. My previous research has focused on graph neural networks, spatiotemporal modeling, explainable AI, and anomaly detection.
