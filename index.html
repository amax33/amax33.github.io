About

About me

I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing at Amirkabir University of Technology, supervised by Dr. Zahed Rahmati and Prof. Amir Goharshady .

I am a member of the ALPACAS research group .

My current research interests include parameterized algorithms, SAT/SMT solving, computational geometry, graph algorithms, and graph neural networks. My previous research has also involved spatiotemporal modeling, explainable AI, and anomaly detection.
