About

I am a PhD student in Artificial Intelligence and Soft Computing at Amirkabir University of Technology, supervised by Dr. Zahed Rahmati and Prof. Amir Goharshady.

My current research interests lie at the intersection of algorithms, formal methods, and graph-based methods, with particular interest in parameterized algorithms, SAT/SMT solving, computational geometry, and graph algorithms. My previous research has focused on graph neural networks, spatiotemporal modeling, explainable AI, and anomaly detection.
